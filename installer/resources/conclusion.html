Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. SpaceDub appears as a delay effect by AE Cobley.

Two things that look like faults and are not:

  • It makes no sound until you switch it on. The Enabled switch starts off, exactly as the 2003 original did. Until it is lit, the plug-in passes audio straight through.
  • Filter Gain starts at 250 %. Also as the original shipped. With resonance up it is loud by design — bring it down before you raise the feedback.

Presets saved by the old Windows DXi cannot be read: the value ranges and the file layout both changed in the port.
